Core Matching Principle

Not Every Truck Going Your Way Is a Match.

A mathematical forward vector check ensures vehicles only receive cargo positioned ahead of their current progress.

Mathematical Forward Vector Engine

D⃗ · H⃗ ≥ 0

Unlike crude geographic aggregators that match any truck in the same district, RoadSide executes vector dot products on the truck's continuous route progress. Backtracking and reverse miles are mathematically eliminated.
