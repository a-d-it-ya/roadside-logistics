The Core Route Matching Principle

Not Every Truck Going Your Way Is a Match.

Standard freight aggregators match any vehicle moving between two cities. RoadSide evaluates the truck’s active remaining route progress using vector dot products to ensure zero backward detours.

POSITION 1 Truck Current Position En Route on Highway
POSITION 2 Forward Pickup Hub Lies AHEAD on Route
POSITION 3 Corridor Destination Continues Forward
INVALID MATCH SCENARIOS (Rejected)
  • ❌
    Truck already passed pickup point:

    Truck is at KM 320; pickup hub was at KM 180.

  • ❌
    Pickup hub lies behind remaining route:

    System rejects hubs requiring backward backtracking.

  • ❌
    Truck would need to reverse direction:

    Violates forward dot product (D · H < 0).

VALID ROADSIDE MATCH (Approved)
  • ✓
    Truck is actively approaching pickup region:

    Sufficient ETA window for cargo to be staged at hub.

  • ✓
    Pickup hub is positioned AHEAD along remaining route:

    Zero km highway detour for the carrier.

  • ✓
    Truck continues seamlessly toward destination:

    Continuous forward corridor flow.
